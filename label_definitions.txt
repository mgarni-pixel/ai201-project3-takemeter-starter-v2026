backed: makes a point and supports it with at least one specific checkable detail, such as a number, price, date, model or spec, named rule or law, link, or quote from a named source
opinion: takes a position (a judgment, recommendation, prediction or factual-sounding claim) but gives nothing checkable beyond the poster's word or general reasoning
chatter: takes no position on the topic, such as a joke, question, thanks, plain agreement, small talk, or a personal story told for its own sake
